Privacy policy

Last updated November 15, 2026 · FocusBoard VR, made by Shlok V.

What this app is. FocusBoard VR is a seated, hands-first productivity space for Meta VR devices. The web pages on this site let you create an account and manage your sticky notes; the VR app loads them on your desk.

What we store. When you register we store your name, email address, and a salted bcrypt hash of your password (never the password itself). When you create or move a note we store its text, color, desk position and rotation, and timestamps.

What we use it for. Only to run the product: showing your notes on your board, restoring their desk spots in VR, and keeping accounts secure. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no selling of data. Nothing is shared with third parties except the hosting provider that runs the app.

Cookies and tracking. This site does not set cookies. Sign-in is kept as a token in your browser's local storage on your device only.

Your controls. From the board page you can export all your data as a JSON file, delete individual notes, and delete your account, which erases your notes permanently. Passwords cannot be recovered, only reset by you.

Guests. The VR experience works without an account. No personal data is collected from guests.

Contact. Questions about this policy? Reach the maker at the about page.
