HANDS-FIRST FOCUS DESK

About

FocusBoard VR, made by Shlok V.

FocusBoard VR is a calm, seated desk in mixed reality built for one thing at a time. You pinch sticky notes off a wooden tray, park a thought where it belongs, and keep a focus ring in your hand while you think. No locomotion, no controllers needed.

It is built with Meta's Immersive Web SDK, runs entirely in the headset's browser, and syncs your board through a small accounts and notes API so your desk is where you left it.

The maker is a Computer and IoT diploma student in Pune who builds calm, focused software. See more of his work at shlok.is-a.dev.
